Structure rules for this plan. (1) Use 8 to 12 sections. Each section covers one specific sub-topic, and its title names that sub-topic in the field's own terms; avoid generic titles such as 'Background' or 'Other findings'. (2) Balance the allocation: no section may receive more than about one sixth of the claims. Split any theme that would exceed this into separate sections. (3) Keep the opening section short: give it only a few framing claims, and do not park findings there. (4) End with a closing section, such as 'Discussion' or 'Conclusions and future directions'. Assign it the claims about limitations, open questions, conflicting results and future work. (5) Word budgets must add up to the target length. Each section's budget should be proportional to its share of the claims.
